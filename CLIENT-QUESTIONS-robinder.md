## A-18, the credentials — verbally, one to one, never in writing

Their workbooks hold roughly **1,200 plaintext credentials**: ImmiAccount logins, clients' Gmail
passwords, security questions, and in `YALE COMPUTER AND PRINTER LOGS`, staff computer PINs and phone
passwords. The `JRP LIST` tab reuses what looks like the same password across ~73 candidates.

**Do not put any of that in a PDF or an email.**

1. A written record that Yale knew about it, addressed to a **Registered Migration Agent**, is
   discoverable. If a client's ImmiAccount is ever misused, that document is the first exhibit.
2. Documents circulate. This one would name the exact tabs, which is a map for anyone who gets it.
3. It reads as an accusation in writing and as help in conversation. Same content, different outcome.

**Raise it on a call, privately, framed as risk not blame:**

> "One thing I noticed, and I'd rather tell you than not — a few of your sheets have client portal
> passwords saved as plain text, including ImmiAccount logins, and one list looks like the same
> password reused across a lot of candidates. Those files get emailed around. I'd change those
> passwords and move them somewhere proper. Happy to show you how — it's about an hour of work."

Then follow up in writing with **the solution only**, never the inventory.
