# Folder contents chart — what goes in each of the 5 sub-folders, by visa type

**Same 5 folders for every matter** (D-100). Folders = *where* documents live. Checklists = *what* is
required, which changes per visa and lives in the tracker (M4). A folder that stays empty for a given visa is
fine and expected (D-102).

Doubles as staff training material for M11.

```
01 Identity & Personal
02 Education & Employment
03 Financial & Sponsorship
04 Forms & Lodgement
05 Correspondence & Outcome
```

---

## 500 — Student
| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| 01 | Passport · national ID · birth certificate · photos · **OSHC policy** · medicals · police check |
| 02 | **CoE** · offer letter · academic transcripts · completion letters · **IELTS/PTE** · previous CoEs |
| 03 | Bank statements · **financial capacity evidence** · tuition receipts · sponsor/parent income & affidavit |
| 04 | **GTE / GS statement** · signed application form · Form 956 · ImmiAccount receipt · TRN |
| 05 | Department letters · **s56 requests** · CoE change correspondence · grant / refusal |

## 820 / 801 — Partner *(and 300 Prospective Marriage)*
| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|

| 02 | Employment evidence for **sponsor** (income, payslips) · applicant's work/study history |

> ⚠️ Relationship evidence spans **01 + 03 + 04** (the Department's four pillars). Workable, but if staff
> prefer one dedicated relationship folder for partner cases, adding a 6th is a ~2-minute change (D-101).

## Fallback rule for any visa not listed
1. Who is the person / what identifies them, plus health & character → **01**
2. What they studied or did for work → **02**
3. Money, and anyone sponsoring them → **03**
4. Anything signed, any Department form, anything lodged → **04**
5. Anything received FROM the Department, and the final result → **05**

**When in doubt: if it came from the Department, it is 05. If we sent it to the Department, it is 04.**
