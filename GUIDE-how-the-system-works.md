## What actually changes for you

**You keep working in a sheet.** That does not change. What changes is what happens *after* you
type a client's name in.

### Today

> You add the client → you make their folder → you work out which checklist → you find it → you
> copy it in → you write the email → you remember to chase them → you hope nothing goes quiet.

### With the system

> You add the client. **The next four steps happen by themselves.** You read the draft email and
> press send.

## Step by step

| # | What happens | Who does it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You add a client — name, visa type, team, office, email | **You** |
| 2 | Their full folder set is created in OneDrive, in the right team's directory, with the right sub-folders for that visa | **System** |
| 3 | The correct checklist is chosen for that visa and filed into their folder | **System** |
| 4 | An email to the client with their checklist is **drafted** in `visa.lodgement@` | **System** |
| 5 | You open the draft, check it, attach the checklist, press send | **You** |
| 6 | Every morning it checks which files have gone quiet | **System** |
| 7 | For a quiet file it **drafts** a follow-up email | **System** |
| 8 | You read that draft and send it | **You** |

**The pattern:** the system does the finding, filing, remembering and typing. **You do the deciding
and the sending.** It never decides anything about a client's application.

## Picking the right checklist

This is the part worth understanding, because it is where your input matters most.

The system chooses from **23 of your own checklists** using three things from the row:

- **Visa type** — 485, 500, 482, and so on
- **Whether anyone else is on the application** — a client with a partner or dependent gets a
  *different* checklist
- **For 485s only — the assessing authority** — TRA, VETASSESS, ACECQA, Engineers Australia, or
  Not required

⚠️ **If any of those three is missing, the system does not guess.** It marks the row
**`NEEDS REVIEW`** and leaves it for a person. It will never file a checklist it is not sure about.

Four visa lines on your current list have no checklist yet — **186, 600, ART and Citizenship**.
Those clients still get their folder and still appear on the dashboard; they are simply flagged for
someone to handle by hand. Robinder and I are discussing covering them properly.

✅ **Partner visas are covered** — there is an 820/801 checklist and the system files it. One row on
your list is written as `PARTNER VISA`, which the system does not recognise. Change it to
**`820/801`** and that client is handled automatically like any other.

## Files that go quiet

Every morning the system looks at every open matter and works out when it was last touched.

- Never contacted → follow up after **3 days**
- Contacted before → follow up after **7 days**
- Anything overdue is **highlighted in the sheet** and appears on the dashboard
- A **chase email listing the outstanding documents is drafted** for you

Enquiries follow your own SOP: **follow up within 7 days, then again after 30**. The moment you
record that someone has replied, **the chasing stops** — a person who has come back to you is a live
conversation, not a cold lead.

⚠️ It flags. It never closes, cancels or writes off a file. Only a person does that.

## The dashboard

One tab, opens on a phone or a laptop, updates itself, **nobody maintains it.**

Across the top, **six headline numbers**: clients on file · open matters · going quiet · granted ·
folders missing · checklists missing.

Then **nine views**:

| | |
|---|---|
| Branch performance | open matters by office and team |
| Where matters are stuck | by processing stage — the biggest number is the bottleneck |
| Workload per consultant | who is carrying the most |
| Going quiet | most overdue first |
| Outcomes | granted and refused |
| Visa mix | what the practice actually works on |
| Documents outstanding | exactly what the chase email lists |
| Blocked on a third party | employer, college, RTO, assessing authority |
| Visa expiry | soonest first, red inside 60 days |

## What it will never do

1. **It will never email a client by itself.** Everything is a draft.
2. **It will never give migration advice.** Only Robinder advises.
3. **It will never guess.** Missing information becomes `NEEDS REVIEW`, not an assumption.
4. **It will never delete or close anything.** It flags; a person decides.
5. **It will never touch a client's password.** Any sheet column named password, username, OTP or
   PIN is excluded from everything the system reads.
